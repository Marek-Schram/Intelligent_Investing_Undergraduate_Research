# Override Log

Any deviation from system output is recorded HERE, BEFORE trading. The count appears as a
headline process metric on every report, and `research/calibration.py` cross-references it.
At the annual review, tally these — this file is the cleanest measurement of whether your
discretionary judgment adds or subtracts value.
